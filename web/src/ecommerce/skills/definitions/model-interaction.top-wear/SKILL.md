# model-interaction.top-wear

## Applicability

Upper-body apparel with product references and an uploaded or AI-created model identity.

## Execution rules

- Preserve the confirmed garment silhouette and construction.
- Use one model identity and wardrobe state across the complete series.
- Assign a distinct camera role to every slot.

## Constraints

- Do not invent garment panels, pockets, buttons, logos or prints.
- Keep hands, shoulders, sleeves and garment contact physically credible.
- Keep a channel-safe text area in every frame.

The structured files beside this document are the executable contract. This document is the reviewable human-readable source.
