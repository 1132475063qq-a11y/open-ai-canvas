# Edit Discipline

Edits operate on the original: keep CHANGE minimal and PRESERVE EXACTLY exhaustive. One main change per pass. Lock identity, wardrobe, props, camera, lighting, shadows, grade and untouched geometry unless explicitly changed.

If a frame needs rebuilding, classify it as regeneration rather than an edit. For location reverse/new views, specify the new camera position and the new left/right/front/back arrangement of every major anchor instead of relying on the phrase “reverse angle.”
