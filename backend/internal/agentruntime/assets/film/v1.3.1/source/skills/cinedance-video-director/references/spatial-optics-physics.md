# Spatial / Optics / Physics

- First frame: required characters are visible immediately unless an empty opening is explicitly intended.
- Blocking: specify screen position, world position, distance to anchor, torso direction, gaze and movement direction.
- Gaze and body orientation are separate constraints.
- Optics: choose by content need; describe visual outcome and guard against focal drift.
- Camera side and action axis must be explicit when reversals would break continuity.
- Physics: weight, contact, inertia, settling motion, liquids, cloth and prop handling must react to action.
- Lighting: preserve one coherent source logic and direction across internal cuts.
