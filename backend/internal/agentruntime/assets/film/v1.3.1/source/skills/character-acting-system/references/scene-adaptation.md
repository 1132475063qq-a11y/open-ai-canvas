# Scene Adaptation

For each present character: define objective, obstacle/stakes, tactic changes, beats and subtext; then express them through center of gravity, tempo, openness, breath, physical business, proxemics/status, listening, eye life and speech rhythm.

A behavior that cannot physically occur in the current posture is transformed rather than deleted. Voice identity stays fixed while acting behavior adapts. Every beat change should be visible in pause, posture, gaze, breath, distance or tempo.
